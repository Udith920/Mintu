FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

No. You can book directly. If you have prescriptions, X-rays or MRI reports, please bring them to your first visit or share them during your online consultation.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition. Most musculoskeletal problems improve within 5–10 sessions. Your physiotherapist will share a plan after your first assessment.

How does a home visit work?

A qualified physiotherapist visits your home with the required equipment. A home-visit charge of ₹500 per visit applies on top of the treatment price.

What payment options are available?

Pay online with UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit or credit cards, or netbanking, all secured by Razorpay. You can also pay at the clinic with cash, UPI or card.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Use the “Manage booking” page with your booking reference and mobile number, free of charge up to 4 hours before your appointment. Refunds for online payments are processed within 5–7 working days.

Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?

Many health insurance policies cover physiotherapy after hospitalisation. We provide itemised invoices for your claims.
